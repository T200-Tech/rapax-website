01 / IDENTITYWHO WE ARE

NOT JUST IDEAS. ENGINEERED OUTCOMES.

Curiosity is the start.
Engineering is the process.

RAPAX is an independent research and development organisation focused on creating practical, reliable and adaptable products, systems and equipment.

We work across disciplines. We identify problems worth solving, investigate what already exists, then develop and test better approaches.

The goal is not complexity for its own sake. It is useful work, sound engineering and products that can be maintained, repaired and improved.
